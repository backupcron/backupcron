Backups on
your schedule.

Back up files, projects and databases on your Mac. Free and open source.

Download for macOS

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

What you can back up

Choose a backup source

Save ordinary copies or ZIP archives. Choose how many recent versions to keep.

Include your Git history and skip dependency folders such as node_modules.

Take SQLite snapshots or create PostgreSQL and MySQL dumps. Check table sizes and choose which tables to keep.

Install on your Mac

Open the downloaded DMG and drag BackupCron into the Applications folder.

Version 1.5 is not notarized by Apple. First launch instructions

Keep BackupCron open to run scheduled backups. You can enable launch at login in the app’s settings.
